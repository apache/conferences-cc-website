Exhibitors

Apache Software Foundation

Founded in June 1999, the Apache Software Foundation provides organizational, legal, and financial support for the Apache open-source software projects. Formerly known as the Apache Group, the Foundation incorporated as a membership-based, not-for-profit corporation to ensure that the Apache projects continue to exist beyond the participation of individual volunteers, to enable contributions of intellectual property and financial support, and to provide a vehicle for limiting legal exposure while participating in open-source projects.

Borders

Borders is a company dedicated to becoming the world's best loved bookstore. With over 300 stores in the nation as well as overseas, Borders is well on its way to doing just that. Each store is supported by over 500,000 titles, most stores offer over 200,000 books, music, DVD and video titles.

Chili!Soft

Chili!Soft, Inc. is a privately held, pioneer provider of enabling technologies and applications for Active Server Pages. Chili!Soft's award-winning flagship product, Chili!Soft ASP, extends the ASP framework to major Web servers and operating systems including Microsoft Windows NT, UNIX, and Linux.

Collab.Net

Collab.Net is the provider of sourceXchange, a new Internet tool that makes it easy for companies to sponsor Open Source projects that address their specific concerns — with guaranteed payment to the developers who work on those projects. And this service is free for developers!

Covalent Technologies

Covalent Technologies is the leader in e-commerce solutions and secured data applications for the Apache Web server platform. Covalent develops commercial software enhancements for Apache, including the Raven™ SSL module, and is the only source for comprehensive commercial support for the Apache Web server platform.

Entrust.net

Entrust.net offers a broad portfolio of certificates and solutions to securely manage e-business transactions and relationships. Entrust.net certificate offerings include Web server SSL, client S/MIME and wireless access protocol (WAP) certificates. Entrust.net delivers premium service and quality trust solutions to organizations offering products and services to global customers via the Web.

IBM Corporation

At IBM, we strive to lead in the creation, development and manufacture of the industry's most advanced information technologies. IBM has an application framework and provides an application server and web design tools that give you the performance and flexibility you want, to build the kind of web sites your companies and customers need. IBM offers partnership programs that provide support and training to help you get going — and growing — fast. IBM is e-business.

LinuxMall

LinuxMall.com is the number one e-commerce site for Linux and one of the 200 busiest sites on the Internet today. LinuxMall.com was created to respond to the needs of the growing Linux Community, and has served the Linux Community since 1996. LinuxMall.com is vendor-neutral and devoted to "all things" Linux.

Lutris Technologies

Lutris Technologies is an Open Source enterprise solutions company and developer and founding sponsor of Enhydra, the primary application server for the Linux community, and the industry's leading Open Source Java/XML application server. Lutris was founded in 1995, and has approximately 100 employees.

O'Reilly Network

The O'Reilly Network is the online source for developers interested in open and emerging technologies, including new platforms, programming languages, and operating systems.

PentaSafe

PentaSafe provides auditing and security management software solutions for the operating systems and applications that are leading the digital economy — including Apache, Windows NT/2000, and Unix systems.

S.u.S.E.

SuSE has offered Linux products since 1992. Operating internationally, SuSE Linux AG is the #1 European distribution and is the choice of over 50,000 business customers worldwide.

Sun Microsystems, Inc.

Since its inception in 1982, a singular vision — The Network Is The Computer™ — has propelled Sun Microsystems, Inc. to its position as a leading provider of industrial-strength hardware, software and services that power the Internet.

UUNET

UUNET, an MCI WorldCom Company and a global leader in Internet communications solutions, offers a comprehensive range of Internet services to business customers worldwide. The UUNET network spans more than 2,000 Points of Presence (POPs) around the globe, with connectivity in over 100 countries and more than 70,000 business customers.

VA Linux Systems

As the first company to market high-performance Linux workstations and servers, VA has become the world's largest and fastest-growing Linux-only provider of systems, software, service and support solutions for the enterprise.

Zend, Inc.

Zend Technologies Ltd. is the leader in PHP technology. We aim to provide commercial viability and backing for PHP-based Web solutions while developing the Zend Engine, an integrated part of PHP.
